# Supplied brand references

crops and any future derivatives. They are not served by the site.

- `lockup-dark.jpg` — 1536 × 838, dark background.
- `lockup-light.jpg` — 1568 × 855, light background.

The crest crops in `public/brand/` were cut from these files, background-removed and
optimised. Do not recolour, distort or redraw the mark. Request vector masters from the
brand team for large-format use.
